What happens in the first minutes

  1. Accident reported

    A citizen or police officer reports it through MEDORA, or the ambulance crew says the location out loud.

  2. Hospitals are alerted

    Every emergency-ready hospital within 40 km sees the ambulance coming, along with how long it will take.

  3. Paramedic reports

    One tap for severity, then ICU, ventilator or surgery if needed. No typing.

  4. Best match is booked

    Travel time, hospital load and free beds are weighed together. The bed is held in a single step that no other ambulance can take.

  5. Route and live location

    The road route is drawn on the same page. The hospital watches the ambulance arrive live and frees the bed after surgery or discharge.
